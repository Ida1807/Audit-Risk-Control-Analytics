# The Business Problem
The project addresses the challenge of analysing audit findings, identifying risk patterns and monitoring corrective actions across multiple branches and business processes. Using Power BI, I consolidated and visualised 208 audit records across four branches to monitor audit completion, risk ratings, audit outcomes and corrective actions, supporting risk-based decision-making and identifying areas requiring further investigation.

# Objectives
What business value does it provide?
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of audit activity and helps users:
- Identify branches and processes with concerning audit outcomes.

- Compare findings and risk indicators across business areas.

- Highlight records that may require further investigation.

- Review recorded corrective actions and redressal percentages.

- Make audit reporting more accessible and support risk-based prioritisation.


# Dataset Overview
The dataset contains 208 audit records across four branches, covering multiple audit periods in 2022. It includes audit status, business processes, risk indicators, audit outcomes, corrective actions and remediation information.

| Category | Description |

|Number of records |	208 audit records |
|Branches |	East, West, North and South |
|Audit periods	| Multiple audit periods in 2022 |
|Business processes	| Branch Operations, Food Processing, Financial Control, Inventory Management, Customer Management |

|Risk information	| Risk Rating, Significance and Instances |
|Audit outcomes	| Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, Needs Significant Improvement and Unsatisfactory |
